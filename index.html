Methodology for chip and SoC teams

Engineers should engineer.

Spec drift and process noise cost chip programs time, budget and re-spins. Xeffective Systems is building the methodology layer that takes process and documentation off engineers, from the first market document to delivery.

Share your pain points The problem we solve

Our goal: streamline the design life cycle, from front end to back end, so engineering teams invest their brain bandwidth in the work that adds value (design, verification testbenches, test code), shortening time to market and reducing bug escapes.

The problem

Specs shouldn't get lost between stages.

  1. No common language

    Marketing, architecture, design, verification and validation each describe the same product in their own documents and terms. Nothing ties them together unambiguously.

  2. Specs drift

    As a project moves from market requirements to architecture, design, verification, bring-up and production, what gets built slowly moves away from what was specified.

  3. Time lost to documents

    Engineers hunt for the right version of the right document, and every stage adds its own intermediate documents to the pile.

What we're building

One source of truth that works the way your team works.

We're building an AI-agent-assisted methodology platform. It keeps every stage of a project working from one source of truth, and it adapts to each customer's own methodology instead of forcing a new one. Our first focus is chip and SoC development.

For engineers and designers

One place to start your work. Less time spent on documents and process, and more time on design, verification and validation.

For engineering managers

A live view of the whole project. See spec status and risks without chasing documents or status meetings.

What we're aiming for

  • Less non-engineering effort
  • Shorter time to market
  • Fewer spec-adherence issues and bugs

Founder

Wlamir Mello

Founder, Xeffective Systems

Wlamir Mello is a seasoned electronic and computer engineer with more than 25 years designing chips and systems, with hands-on and leadership experience across the full product life cycle, in semiconductors, medical devices, industrial automation and nuclear research.

His work spans the stack: high-speed analog and mixed-signal hardware, embedded and real-time software, FPGA prototyping and emulation, and ASIC/SoC verification, validation and silicon bring-up. He has led engineering as R&D manager, engineering director and senior technical architect, worked directly with customers in technical account management and marketing, and taught electrical circuits, power electronics and programming.

Across every role, one pattern stood out: teams lose a large share of their time to documentation and process instead of engineering. He has spent his career analyzing how engineering work really flows and building methodologies, including concurrent engineering and Agile, that let teams focus on design. Xeffective Systems is where that experience becomes a platform.
