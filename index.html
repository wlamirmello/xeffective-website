The problem

Specs shouldn't get lost between stages.

  1. No common language

    Marketing, architecture, design, verification and validation each describe the same product in their own documents and terms. Nothing ties them together unambiguously.

  2. Specs drift

    As a project moves from market requirements to architecture, design, verification, bring-up and production, what gets built slowly moves away from what was specified.

  3. Time lost to documents

    Engineers hunt for the right version of the right document, and every stage adds its own intermediate documents to the pile.

What we're building

One source of truth that works the way your team works.

We're building an AI-agent-assisted methodology platform. It keeps every stage of a project working from one source of truth, and it adapts to each customer's own methodology instead of forcing a new one. Our first focus is chip and SoC development.

One place to start your work. Less time spent on documents and process, and more time on design, verification and validation.

For engineering managers

A live view of the whole project. See spec status and risks without chasing documents or status meetings.

What we're aiming for

  • Less non-engineering effort
  • Shorter time to market
  • Fewer spec-adherence issues and bugs
